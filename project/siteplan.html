Guely Studio

Guely Studio is a handmade bookbinding business that creates personalized embroidered covers, planners, and notebooks.

I chose the name "Guely Studio" because it represents the handmade business and gives it a simple and memorable identity.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Guely Studio website is to showcase handmade bookbinding products and personalized embroidered covers. The website will help visitors learn about the products, view examples of previous work, and contact Guely Studio to request a custom product or place an order.

The website will include product photographs, descriptions, customization options, and contact information.

Scenarios

  • How can I order a personalized embroidered cover for my scriptures?
  • What types of handmade planners and notebooks does Guely Studio offer?
  • Can I request a custom design or choose different embroidery colors?

Typography

Georgia

Georgia will be used for the main headings and section titles. It gives the website a traditional and handmade feeling.

Arial

Arial will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, navigation, and other general text because it is simple and easy to read.
